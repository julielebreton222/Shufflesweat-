# Shuffle Sweat

A home-workout card deck for brains that get bored. Cards flip one at a time, rounds are short, an "I'm bored" button swaps in a wildcard, and stopping early always counts as a win. Weekly targets come from WHO, ACSM and current strength-training research.

It's a plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript app that installs on an iPhone Home Screen and works offline. Everything is saved on the device only.

- **Run it locally:** `python3 -m http.server 8000`, then open http://localhost:8000

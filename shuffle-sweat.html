Where these numbers come from
  • WHO 2020 and US 2018 guidelines: 75–150 min of vigorous activity a week, or 150–300 moderate, plus strength work for all major muscle groups on 2+ days. More than that brings extra benefit. Short bouts count, there's no 10-minute minimum. US guidelines · WHO summary
  • Pelland et al., Sports Medicine 2025 (67 studies): more weekly hard sets means more muscle and strength, with smaller extra gains as you add more. Training a muscle twice a week helps strength. Paper
  • ACSM: strength 2–3 days a week with about 48 hours between sessions for the same muscles. Flexibility 2–3 days a week, daily is better, about 60 seconds per muscle group. ACSM summary
  • Stamatakis et al., Nature Medicine 2022: even three 1–2 minute vigorous bursts a day were linked to much lower mortality in non-exercisers. A 5-minute round is worth doing. Study
  • How the app counts: vigorous minutes are Burn, Athlete and all-out cards plus the short rests between them. A strength day is 6+ hard sets. A mobility day is 90+ seconds of mobility cards.
